The egg is ready.

I built Megg — a mobile-first boiled-egg timer that finds the exact cook time from the egg’s weight, starting temperature, and yolk doneness. Then it counts down and rings when it’s done.

Just the way you like it.

• Five sizes, Small to Jumbo
• Fridge or room temperature
• Runny, jammy, firm, or hard-boiled
• Portuguese, English, and German
• Install it on your phone so the alarm still works with the screen locked

Try it: https://megg-zeta.vercel.app
Code: https://github.com/leovenom/Megg

Built with Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and Motion.
